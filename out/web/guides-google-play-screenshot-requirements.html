Google Play Store Screenshot Requirements & Sizes (2026)

Publishing an Android app on Google Play Console? Google offers flexible aspect ratios compared to Apple, but enforces strict minimum dimensions, maximum ratios, and tablet coverage requirements for store featuring.

Recommended Google Play Screenshot Resolutions

Device Category Aspect Ratio Recommended Resolution Format & Max Size
Modern Android Phones 9:19.5 (Pixel 9 Pro / Galaxy S24) 1080 × 2340 px or 1080 × 2400 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (Max 8 MB)
Standard HD Android 9:16 (Universal Standard) 1080 × 1920 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (Max 8 MB)
QHD+ Flagships 9:20 (High-Res Android) 1440 × 3120 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (Max 8 MB)
7-Inch Tablets 16:10 or 16:9 1200 × 1920 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (Max 8 MB)
10-Inch Tablets 16:10 (Large Tablet) 1600 × 2560 px or 2048 × 1536 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (Max 8 MB)

Google Play Console Submission Checklist

  • Minimum & Maximum Dimensions: Minimum dimension is 320 px; maximum dimension is 3840 px.
  • Aspect Ratio Constraint: The longer side of your screenshot cannot be more than twice the length of the shorter side (max aspect ratio 2:1 or 1:2).
  • Quantity: Upload between 2 and 8 screenshots per device category. Google recommends uploading at least 4 screenshots to maximize conversion.
  • Tablet Requirement for Featured Placement: To qualify for "Designed for tablets" badging and editorial featuring, you must upload at least 4 screenshots for both 7-inch and 10-inch tablets.
