Apple App Store Screenshot Rules & Requirements

  • Format: High-quality PNG or JPEG. 72 DPI, sRGB or Display P3 color profile.
  • No Alpha Channel: PNG files MUST NOT contain transparency or alpha layers. Flatten all graphics before upload.
  • Screenshot Limits: Up to 10 screenshots per device family. The first 3 screenshots appear in organic App Store search results.
  • Orientation: All screenshots in a set must share the same orientation (portrait or landscape).
  • Content Guidelines: Screenshots must showcase the actual in-app experience. Avoid pricing promises, misleading mockups, or claims prohibited by App Store Review Guideline 2.3.
